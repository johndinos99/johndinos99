## Hi, I'm Ioannis

I'm a Full Stack Developer based in Athens, Greece, with a BSc in Informatics & Telematics from Harokopio University of Athens.

I enjoy building web applications from the ground up, working across the backend, frontend, databases, and deployment. Most of my experience comes from personal and university projects where I've had the chance to build real applications.
